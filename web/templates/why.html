beyond the chain · project delusional

A plant does not want a token. It wants a name and a boring bill.

If you work in operations, energy, or automation, skip the ticker. The useful objects here are the same ones you already have on a line or a substation: identity, a way to call something, and a cost that does not jump when a market does.

What maps, without pretending we plugged into SCADA

A name for a thing that acts

zenon (COPA-DATA) already names equipment, drivers, and pictures of a plant. This site names an agent or a service on Kaspa: line3.kas as a handle a machine can resolve, call, and pay. It is not a zenon project. It is the same human need: do not talk to a 60-character address.

A prepaid unit of work

Industrial systems meter telegrams, not dollars. Gramlane meters grams of sequenced work. One job, one gram bill. That is closer to a prepaid message budget than to USDC. Kaspa Till is for when the invoice really is money — and that dollar is not live on L1 yet.

An agent that can be delegated

OT is growing an AI layer on top of SCADA. An agent still needs a public identity and a way to pay for inclusion. Web4.0 here = readable, discoverable, callable, payable. No browser required.
