What this is.

Three local Kaspa L1 sites. Names, agents, and fees — without faking a dollar and without an L2. This tab is the explanation. You are on KNS (http://127.0.0.1:8080).

The idea

Other chains use stables so dApp costs and price tags stay boring. Stables need capital. Kaspa covenants can lock work. They cannot mint USD. So we split the problem:

1 · Names

This site. A .kas name from the live KNS indexer. Agents get a card, MCP, and a Kaspa 402. Uniqueness is indexer FCFS, not L1 consensus.

2 · Fees that do not surprise

Gramlane :8081 bills sequenced jobs in grams (Work Credits). 1 credit = 1 KIP-21 gram. Not a stablecoin.

3 · A shelf that waits for a real L1 dollar

Kaspa Till :8082 prices goods in reserved kUSD (not live). Today you pay KAS at a merchant sign. No fake peg.

What this URL does

Resolve kns.kas, show owner and pay URI, build an agent card, ask for payment with HTTP 402. Connect Kasware or Kastle. Log out anytime. KaChat / Kassword / ranks are pointers — we do not encrypt, store secrets, or look up NFTs.
