Kassword · Toccata vault · @Kas_Ranks

A name can point at a vault. It cannot open it.

kassword.com is a browser-only post-quantum vault (Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305, ML-KEM-1024, ML-DSA-87) with optional encrypted DAG backup and a Toccata covenant locker. There is no Kassword server. This app does not store passwords.

What to bind on a name

A vaultCommit (hash of the sealed blob) and a pointer “open Kassword, this wallet”. File bytes stay in the browser / DAG ciphertext.

What this app will not do

No master password field. No ciphertext upload. No “recover for me”. That would be a Kassword server, which they refuse to be.

Locker branches

Kassword 2.0: Schnorr, HLMT cold, escrow, dead-man, M-of-N — enforced by Toccata, not by us. Spec the spend rule on the name later; do not fake it here.
